- [AlphaGenome 2: deciphering how cells read DNA to better understand disease](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/)
  Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a model that takes a 1-Mb DNA sequence plus a target tissue, cell type or cell state and predicts gene regulation and the effects of genetic variants in that setting. It was trained on human and mouse single-cell atlases and bulk functional genomics data, and it can be applied to new single-cell datasets without fine-tuning. It also predicts DNA methylation. The preprint (Gayoso, Mottram, Karollus and colleagues, with Hassabis, Kohli and Avsec as senior authors: https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/DeepMind.com/Blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/alphagenome-2.pdf) reports that it doubles recall of causal single-cell eQTLs at 90 percent precision compared with earlier models. It also comes with a precomputed atlas of enhancer-gene links and scores for 7 million common variants. Access is through an API for non-commercial research. Found through a LinkedIn post by Amanda Stafford (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/amanda-stafford-phd-059325152_today-we-are-excited-to-launch-alphagenome-share-7526304596818837504-W9Ny/). For the beat, the angle is how researchers get access to it: a closed model behind a non-commercial API with a precomputed atlas, and AlphaGenome is one of the databases in the Antigravity science plugin covered in No. 032.
