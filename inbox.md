- [freephdlabor: open-source multiagent framework for autonomous scientific research](https://github.com/ltjed/freephdlabor)
  GitHub repository by user ltjed. MIT-licensed Python multiagent framework that runs a ManagerAgent orchestrating specialized subagents (IdeationAgent, WriteupAgent, and others) to take a research direction and produce a paper with experiments, figures, and citations. Repo claims to be "the first multiagent system to produce peer-reviewed research end-to-end," accompanied by a technical report at arXiv 2510.15624 and a blog at freephdlabor.github.io. 662 stars, 75 forks as of August 30 2026. Positions itself against closed systems on four axes. Dynamic workflows that adapt in real time, full customization (add or remove agents), integrated human-in-the-loop, and continual (context-managed) research rather than one-off runs. Relevance. Fits into the AI-scientist tools thread as the open-source, community-facing counterpart to the frontier-lab bundles now consolidating as a category (Google Co-Scientist in draft 023, the Claude Team plan for scientists in this inbox above, ChatGPT for Academic Researchers in edition 017, Biomni and Robin earlier in the year). It also creates useful tension with the Terminal-Bench Science result in draft 023 (agents currently pass about 30 percent of real research tasks), and freephdlabor sits at the aspirational end of the same axis Terminal-Bench measures.
