- [ResearchPigeon: a daily arXiv digest picked by embedding similarity](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/charliemcbs_last-week-i-launched-researchpigeon-a-personalised-share-7522234396184104960-EJ8m/)
  A new free tool (researchpigeon.com) that emails one arXiv paper a day, chosen by comparing an embedding of the reader's written interests with embeddings of new submissions. Likes and dislikes move the profile. The launch post says arXiv's AI category grew more than sixfold in two years and gets nearly 200 papers a day, which is the reason it exists: AI research output is now large enough that readers hand the first filter to another model. - [Summit on Math Research in the Age of AI: report](https://cmsa.fas.harvard.edu/aimathresearch_summit/)
  32 mathematicians met at Harvard's CMSA on 22 and 23 October (organizers Freed, Guth, Kra, Riehl, Abouzaid) and published recommendations on how AI is changing math research: credit and authorship for AI-assisted work, formalization in Lean as a supplement to the literature, training early-career mathematicians, and new publication formats (interactive articles, databases, venues that separate dissemination from validation). Names Hexagon and Palomar as new venues and the Bourgain Project, Erdős Problems and LMFDB as new ways to organize math. Report PDF: https://cmsa.fas.harvard.edu/media/2026/10/Math-research-summit-report.pdf
