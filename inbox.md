- [Terminal-Bench Science: a benchmark for AI agents on real scientific workflows](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/stevendillmann_were-releasing-terminal-bench-science-a-share-7498837692424548352-Ktsm/)
  Steven Dillmann (Stanford, Laude Institute) released Terminal-Bench Science, a community benchmark of 70 terminal-based research tasks across five domains (life, physical, earth, and mathematical sciences, plus engineering), each adapted by a working researcher from their own real workflow rather than a toy problem. - [Accelerating Scientific Research with Gemini in the Real-World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26701)
  Samuel Schmidgall, Tao Tu, and a large Google team (including Quoc V. Le and Vivek Natarajan), arXiv, submitted August 27 2026. Describes Co-Scientist, a Gemini-powered multi-agent system that runs full research cycles across materials science, biology, and computer science, from hypothesis generation through experiments (including physically driving lab equipment) to writing up a manuscript. The headline claim is that it autonomously found an inference-time scaling architecture that beat six frontier models on HealthBench while lowering potential clinical harm. Why it matters for the beat: this is the maximal version of the AI-as-research-operator thread from edition 022 (Claude's protein-design run, the Inherent replication agent), a first-party claim of closed-loop autonomous discovery, and it lands right next to the Terminal-Bench Science result already in this inbox showing agents still pass only about 30 percent of real research tasks. Good tension to hold side by side.
