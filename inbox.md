- [Simons Foundation Launches $15M Effort To Help Mathematicians Navigate AI Era](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/news/simons-foundation-launches-15m-effort-to-help-mathematicians-navigate-ai-era/)
  Simons Foundation press release, October 15 2026, shared by Terence Tao on his blog (https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/15/simons-foundation-launches-15m-effort-to-help-mathematicians-navigate-ai-era/). The Simons Foundation, Anthropic and OpenAI are putting $15 million in combined funding and resources into workshops and other work that helps mathematicians evaluate AI-assisted results, learn new tools and develop shared standards for AI use. The foundation manages the money, including through an open call for proposals, and the two companies also provide AI tools and compute to foundation-supported researchers, including in basic science outside math. David Spergel (foundation president) says the community, not any single company, should decide how these tools are evaluated and adopted, which sits awkwardly next to two AI companies co-funding it.
