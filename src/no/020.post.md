A Fields medalist says he doesn't want to train students for a career in mathematics that may no longer exist. Two teams posted AI-generated proofs of the same crypto problem three hours apart. And a study found that frontier AI agents can automate every part of research engineering but can't answer an open research question.

Three editions of The Hallway Track running, AI-in-math results keep accumulating. But the gap between "solve a known open problem when pointed at it" and "pursue a novel research question" turns out to be wide. This week's edition maps that gap, plus new tools for evidence synthesis and a publishing system straining under 33% more submissions.
