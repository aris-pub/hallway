A PNNL team argues that every AI-scientist tool shipped this year (Biomni, Co-Scientist, Robin) scales individual reasoners. But science is team work. Their system, Mycelium, routes insights across researchers and AI agents in a shared workspace instead.

Meanwhile this week: the US committed $5B+ to AI-for-science across 15 federal agencies, ARIA backed a directory of 123 expert-submitted problems for AI researchers to solve, and a new paper argues peer reviewers should be *allowed* to upload manuscripts to AI, contrarian to the 93% of medical journals that prohibit it.

The institutions are no longer arguing about whether AI belongs in science. They are arguing about which layer it should occupy.
