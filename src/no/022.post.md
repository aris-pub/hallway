Terry Tao, the Fields medalist, just helped launch a new kind of preprint server. Palomar is a registry for Lean-verified mathematical proofs. Each submission must typecheck, contain no extra axioms, and match its informal statement.

It exists because AI systems are now producing formalized proofs faster than anyone can manually check them. The registry automates the checking: submit your repo, and Palomar verifies whether it actually proves what it claims. Not a policy document. Not a disclosure requirement. Working infrastructure that runs the proof.
