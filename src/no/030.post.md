A study released this week analyzed 226,000 US STEM PhD dissertations filed between 2019 and 2026. In 2022, AI-generated writing appeared in essentially none of them. By 2026, it appears in 29%.

The career finding is the part worth sitting with: graduates whose dissertations include AI writing are 17% less likely to enter academic research careers. Whether that reflects how those students trained, what skills they developed or did not develop, or something else about the population is not answered by the data. But the correlation is large and consistent across fields, from math and engineering down through life and physical sciences.
