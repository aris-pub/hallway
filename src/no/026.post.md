A Google and Google DeepMind study based on 15 million AI interactions finds scientists save an average of 7 hours a week. But 41 percent report a growing backlog of untested hypotheses.

That's the finding I keep thinking about. The time savings are real. The bottleneck didn't disappear, it moved. Hours freed from literature search and drafting are now spent checking AI outputs and queuing up results that haven't been verified yet. The constraint is no longer access to tools, it's the capacity to test what the tools produce.
