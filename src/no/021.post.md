Litvak planted 100 errors across 10 psychology papers and ran them through Claude, GPT-5.5, Gemini, Reviewer3, and Refine.ink. The best single system caught 71. The worst caught 30. But the ensemble caught 93, because the systems are surprisingly uncorrelated in which errors they find.

The 7 that slipped through every system were all omissions: missing information, not wrong information. AI reviewers are good at spotting what's broken. They struggle to notice what isn't there.
